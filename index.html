Just In · Phase 3 Read-out
May 08, 2026 · 23:45 IST

Phase 3 clinical studies of EdoMab™ tildrakizumab-edf 100 mg in active psoriatic arthritis meet their primary endpoint

Edolf has reported that the twin Phase 3 clinical studies (EDO-PsARC-1 and EDO-PsARC-2) evaluating EdoMab™ tildrakizumab-edf 100 mg in adults with active psoriatic arthritis have met their primary endpoint — statistically significant improvement in ACR20 response at week 24 versus placebo, with key secondary endpoints (ACR50, PASI 75, MDA, HAQ-DI) also positive and a safety profile consistent with the established psoriasis label. The Phase 3 read-out is the underpinning evidence for the EdoMab™ sBLA accepted by the USFDA earlier today, and detailed data will be shared at the 2026 EULAR Annual Congress and in a peer-reviewed publication.

May 8 · DCGI Approval
May 08, 2026 · 18:00 IST

CDSCO/DCGI approves EdoSema™ — Edolf's generic semaglutide injection in India, launching under the EdoTrim™ and SemaEdo™ sub-brands

India's Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation has granted Edolf manufacturing and marketing approval for EdoSema™, a once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide. The molecule will reach Indian patients under two India sub-brands: EdoTrim™ for adults with obesity or overweight and weight-related comorbidities, and SemaEdo™ for adults living with type-2 diabetes. Commercial launch begins in Q2 FY27 through a tiered-pricing programme designed to make GLP-1 metabolic therapy accessible across Tier-1 and Tier-2 markets, with paired EdoSpiro™ respiratory-comorbidity screening at every Edolf wellness centre. EdoSema™ joins a broader GLP-1 platform that includes EdoTirz™ (tirzepatide; SURPASS-EDO Phase 3 read-out due H2 FY27).
