About Edolf

Caring for Life,
One Patient at a Time

Edolf Pharmacy is a fast-scaling global pharmaceutical company developing, manufacturing, and distributing medicines that meaningfully improve health outcomes — from chronic disease essentials to specialty therapies in oncology, dermatology, women's health, and metabolic disorders.

Built on a vertically integrated model spanning APIs, formulations, biosimilars and consumer healthcare, we are committed to making innovation affordable and access universal — caring for life across every step of the patient journey.

✓USFDA, EMA & WHO-GMP approved manufacturing across 13 sites
✓10% of revenue invested annually in R&D and biosimilars
✓Patient assistance reaching 5.2M+ people across 65 countries in FY26
