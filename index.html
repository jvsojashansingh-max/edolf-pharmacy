Just In · USFDA Label Update
May 08, 2026 · 16:00 IST

USFDA approves the EdoCosi™ (cosibelimab-edf) label update based on longer-term clinical-outcomes data in advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (aCSCC)

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has approved a label update for EdoCosi™ (cosibelimab-edf) — the anti-PD-L1 monoclonal antibody Edolf made available in the United States this week for advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma. The update reflects longer-term clinical-outcomes data from the pivotal aCSCC trial, including updated objective-response and duration-of-response rates, longer median follow-up, and refreshed safety information across both metastatic and locally-advanced cohorts. The new prescribing information strengthens the EdoCosi™ clinical profile for community oncologists and academic cancer centres, supports continued $0-first-fill patient access, and is the second EdoCosi™ milestone inside a single calendar week — alongside the broader US availability launch. The combined US aCSCC market remains estimated at $700M+ annually and continues to be underserved by current PD-1/PD-L1 options.
